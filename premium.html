What is arbitrage? ▼
Arbitrage happens when different sportsbooks offer prices that allow a guaranteed profit.

These opportunities are rare and can disappear quickly as sportsbooks update their odds.

When available, compare both prices and act quickly before the market adjusts.

▶ Why line value matters
A team can still be favored to win and be a poor bet at the current odds.
We evaluate the value of the line — not just who is likely to win.
